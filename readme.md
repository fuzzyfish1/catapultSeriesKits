# Catapultkits

The original idea was to create a series of themed kits centered around catapults as a fun and intuitive way to teach physics concepts. The plan includes four kits: the first three focus on different medieval-style launching mechanisms, and the fourth provides a target to complete the set. When assembled together, the four kits form a playable game that students can take home and enjoy.

The first kit features a Da Vinci–style catapult with one controllable variable—arm length—allowing students to explore basic experimentation and the scientific method. The second kit introduces two variables in a trebuchet—counterweight mass and arm length—to deepen their understanding. The third kit adds a third layer of complexity, using a rubber band launcher with adjustable number of bands, preload (initial stretch), and arm length.

By progressively increasing the number of controllable variables, each kit encourages hands-on discovery of different physics principles and reinforces the process of scientific experimentation. The final kit focuses on gamification, serving both as a fun conclusion to the series and as a bridge to spark interest in more advanced classes—especially those involving Arduino and other STEM topics.

This series teaches physics + scientific method + assembly and motor skills + tries to get kids interested in our more engineeringy courses at the end by gamifying this

## teaching timeline

1. Day 1:
	* Davinci Catapult - 1 DOF
	* Engineering principles
		- levers
		- mechanical advantage
		- moment of inertia
		- hookes law (concept only)
		- scientific method + hypothesis testing
	* have them hypothesis abt how arm length changes shot distance
	* have them conduct an experiment recording distances for how far everything launches at every arm length
	* let them fly the catapults and record some lengths, come back together to share their findings and see who is right
	* 1 DOF, arm is adjustable
2. Day 2
	* Trebuchet - 2 DOF
	* engineering principles
		- Energy + Transfer
		- potential energy
		- impulse momentum theorem
	* have them write down hypotheses and figure it out same as before
3. Day 3
	* Rubber band catapult - 3 DOF
	* engineering principles
		- hookes law (again but different)
		- preload
	* have them figure out how to shoot the farthest
4. Day 4
	* use this day as a bit of fun
	* build a target kit
	* use that arduino kit to make a game
	* finish off just playing around
